Who's Lending a Hand?

Shipwreck City has been shaped by the kindness of individuals and organizations across the Seattle maritime community — people who opened their docks, lent their vessels, wrote letters on our behalf, and introduced us to the right people at the right time. This page is a record of that generosity.

Letters of Support

These letters represent formal endorsements from Seattle's maritime, cultural, and environmental community. Each organization has reviewed our work firsthand and chosen to stand behind it — lending credibility, opening doors, and strengthening the case that this archive matters.

About the Project

Shipwreck City is a volunteer-led effort to document, film, and archive every sunken vessel resting on the floor of Seattle's Lake Union and adjacent waterways. Using ROV technology — underwater robots equipped with cameras and side-scan sonar — the team systematically locates, films, and catalogs each target and publishes all findings openly online.

The project builds on a 2017 side-scan sonar survey by Coastal Sensing and Survey, which first mapped approximately 100 submerged targets in Lake Union, as well as historical efforts by DCS Films and the Lake Union Virtual Museum. Shipwreck City's mission is to visit every target, capture high-resolution video of the never-before-seen wrecks and debris, and preserve the historical record for public awareness and use.

All data, photography, and footage are published at ShipwreckCity.org as they are acquired, at no cost to the public. The project actively seeks donations to fund their efforts.
